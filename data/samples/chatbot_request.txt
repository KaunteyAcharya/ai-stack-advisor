KNOWLEDGE BASE EXCERPTS (retrieved for this question)

[1] Delivery times and charges
Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days to metro cities and 5–8 working days elsewhere. Express delivery (metro cities only) takes 1–2 working days and costs ₹149. Standard delivery is free on orders above ₹999; below that it costs ₹79. Live plants are shipped Monday to Wednesday only, so they do not sit in a warehouse over the weekend. Orders placed after 2 pm are processed the next working day. Customers receive a tracking link by SMS and email once the order is packed. During monsoon months (June to September), deliveries to some pin codes in Kerala, Assam and coastal Karnataka may take two extra days.

[2] Plant arrival guarantee
Every live plant is covered by a 7-day arrival guarantee. If a plant arrives damaged, wilted beyond recovery or with pests, the customer should send a photo within 48 hours of delivery through the "Report a problem" button on the order page or by replying to this chat. We replace the plant free of charge or refund the plant price, at the customer's choice. Minor leaf damage during transit is normal and usually recovers within a week with the care steps in the box. Pots and accessories are covered by a separate 30-day return policy and must be unused and in original packaging.

[3] Care for low-light plants
Snake plants, ZZ plants and pothos tolerate low light and suit offices and north-facing rooms. Water when the top 5 cm of soil is dry, usually every 10–14 days in summer and every 3 weeks in winter. Overwatering is the most common cause of yellow leaves and root rot. Use the self-watering pots only for pothos and peace lilies, not for snake plants or ZZ plants, which prefer drier soil. Wipe leaves monthly to remove dust. Fertilise once a month from March to September with the Leafline liquid feed at half strength.
